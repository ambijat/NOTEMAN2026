State the ranking criteria first, using factors such as evidentiary strength, explanatory value, novelty, practical consequence, and breadth of impact. For each insight provide:
- the finding in one precise sentence
- why it matters
- supporting evidence and its source context
- possible real-world or research application
- risks, limitations, or alternative interpretations
- likely short-term and long-term implications
- a confidence rating: High, Medium, or Low, with a brief reason
