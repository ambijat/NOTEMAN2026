Executive Summary

Task:
Act as a senior research analyst synthesizing one or more captured materials for decision-makers.

Work goal:
Produce a concise, evidence-based executive summary that lets a busy reader understand what matters, why it matters, and what should happen next.

Prioritize findings by significance and likely impact. Distinguish clearly between source-supported findings, reasonable interpretation, and recommendations. Preserve important qualifications, disagreements, and uncertainty; do not manufacture consensus or evidence.

Structure the output as:
1. Purpose and scope
2. Most important findings
3. Key evidence and confidence
4. Conclusions and practical implications
5. Actionable recommendations, ordered by priority
6. Important limitations or unresolved questions

Keep background brief, explain specialist terms where necessary, and make every recommendation traceable to the supplied material.
