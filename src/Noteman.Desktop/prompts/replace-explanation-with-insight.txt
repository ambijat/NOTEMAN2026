Replace Explanation With Insight
Group: Human Writing

Task:
Review the captured text and replace obvious explanation with meaningful insight.

Work goal:
Produce a tighter version where each paragraph reveals, challenges, questions, compares, or reframes.

For every paragraph, ask:
Is this explaining something obvious, or offering a meaningful insight?

Remove content that merely explains. Keep content that reveals, challenges, questions, compares, or reframes.

The final version should contain fewer explanations and more insights. Readers should finish the piece feeling they learned something they did not already know.
